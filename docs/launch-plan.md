# Launch Plan: First 7 Days

## Objective

Validate whether people who earn decent money but feel broke before payday will trade an email address and then money for an accountant-backed paycheck system.

## Day 1: Ship The Lead Magnet

- Publish the landing page.
- Connect the email form.
- Upload the Money Leak Finder workbook.
- Add the delivery email from `marketing/email-sequence.md`.

## Day 2: Create The Checkout

- Create a $29 starter product: Paycheck Reset Starter Kit.
- Include the free calculator, bill calendar, subscription audit, debt payoff starter, and a 20-minute walkthrough video.
- Use Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Stripe Payment Links, or Shopify Starter.

## Day 3: Authority Content

Publish three posts:

- "Your checking account is lying to you."
- "Payday should not feel good for only 48 hours."
- "The smallest money leaks are usually the quietest."

Each post should point to the free calculator.

## Day 4: CPA Trust Builder

Record a screen-share walkthrough of the calculator:

- Show one example paycheck.
- Split bills, debt, safety, and life money.
- Explain that it is educational guidance, not individualized financial advice.

## Day 5: Direct Outreach

Send the calculator to 25 people who are likely to know busy professionals, couples, parents, or friends trying to get their money together.

Message:

> I built a free Money Leak Finder for people who make decent money but still feel broke before payday. It shows where the paycheck is going and what to move first. If you know someone who could use it, would you send it their way?

## Day 6: Paid Offer Push

Email the first leads:

- Deliver useful tip.
- Share the payday-vanishing story.
- Offer the $29 starter kit.

## Day 7: Decision Review

Check:

- Landing page visits.
- Opt-in rate.
- Email replies.
- Starter kit sales.
- Questions people ask.

Decision rules:

- If opt-in rate is weak, sharpen the headline and promise.
- If opt-ins happen but sales do not, strengthen the bridge from calculator to paid kit.
- If replies mention a different urgent pain, adjust the product before building more.

## Tools To Connect Next

- Email platform: ConvertKit, Beehiiv, MailerLite, or Kit.
- Payment platform: Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Stripe, or Shopify.
- Deployment: Vercel for the landing page.
- Analytics: Plausible, Fathom, Google Analytics, or Vercel Web Analytics.

## Immediate Build Queue

1. Connect landing page form.
2. Create checkout page/product.
3. Expand workbook into paid starter kit.
4. Record walkthrough video.
5. Publish first three content posts.
6. Deploy landing page publicly.
