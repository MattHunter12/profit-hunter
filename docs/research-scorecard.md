## Why Paycheck Peace Wins

The paycheck product is the best first move because the pain is frequent, emotionally clear, and easy to demonstrate with a spreadsheet.

People do not wake up wanting a budget. They wake up annoyed that payday came and went and nothing feels different. The free Money Leak Finder gives quick relief. The paid system expands the same promise into a repeatable habit.
