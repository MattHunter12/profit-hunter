# Step 17: Launch Traffic Plan

## Goal

Get the first real people, outside of testing, to download the Money Leak Finder and reply with what surprised them.

This step is not about looking big. It is about finding proof that the promise is clear enough for someone to trade an email address for the workbook.

## Primary Link

Use this landing page link anywhere we are asking people to download the workbook:

https://profit-hunter-ourhunterbrand-5062s-projects.vercel.app/

Use the direct download page only after someone subscribes or if you are manually helping a tester:

https://profit-hunter-ourhunterbrand-5062s-projects.vercel.app/download.html

## Launch Position

Core message:

> You can earn decent money and still feel broke before payday because your checking account is not showing bill timing, subscriptions, debt pressure, and true flex cash in one view.

Authority angle:

> As an accountant/CPA, I see this pattern constantly: the income looks fine, but the paycheck has no operating system.

Avoid:

- Tax language.
- Shame around spending.
- Generic "financial freedom" language.
- Making the workbook sound like a complete paid system.

## Metrics To Track

Track these manually once per day during launch:

| Metric | Target for first 10 days | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Landing page visits | 100+ | Enough traffic to learn from behavior. |
| New subscribers | 10+ | Early signal that the promise is clear. |
| Opt-in rate | 20%+ | If lower, headline or CTA may need work. |
| Email replies | 3+ | Best product research at this stage. |
| Conversations started | 10+ | Comments, DMs, replies, texts, calls. |

## Channel Strategy

Primary public channel: LinkedIn personal profile.

Why: The offer needs trust, practical authority, and a working-adult audience. LinkedIn is the cleanest place to test accountant-backed cash-flow content without needing entertainment-first production.

Secondary channels:

- Instagram: good for short-form education, screenshots, and casual story-style authority.
- X/Twitter: good for short, repeatable cash-flow observations and threads.
- Direct outreach: best early conversion channel because it starts from relationship, not algorithm.

Optional later:

- Facebook personal profile: only if you want to warm up people who already know you there.
- TikTok: do not create a TikTok account just for this launch. Reuse winning Instagram Reels there later if the video format starts working.

Do not start with a carousel. Carousels take longer to create, and we do not yet know which angle deserves design time.

## Format Order

1. Text post with link.
   - Fastest to publish.
   - Best for testing the promise.
   - Use the link in the post because the goal is subscribers, not vanity reach.

2. Screenshot post.
   - Use the Dashboard or Paycheck Map.
   - Shows this is a real tool, not just advice.
   - Caption should explain one specific problem the workbook solves.

3. X/Twitter thread.
   - 5 to 7 short posts.
   - One idea per post.
   - Put the landing page link in the final post.

4. Instagram Reel.
   - 30 to 45 seconds.
   - Hook in the first 2 seconds: "Your checking account is not your spending limit."
   - Use captions on screen.
   - CTA: "Comment LEAK or grab the free workbook at the link."

5. Direct outreach.
   - Send to people who know working adults, parents, couples, salespeople, and professionals.
   - Ask them to share it with one person who might need it.

6. Carousel.
   - Build only after one written post or video clearly wins.
   - Best first carousel topic: "5 reasons your paycheck disappears before payday."
