# Content Ideas

## Short-Form Hooks

1. Your checking account is lying to you. Here is why.
2. Payday should not feel good for only 48 hours.
3. You do not need a prettier budget. You need a paycheck map.
4. The smallest money leaks are usually the quietest.
5. I am a CPA/accountant. Here is the cash-flow mistake I keep seeing.
6. You can earn decent money and still feel broke before payday.
7. Before you cut every fun purchase, check your bill timing.
8. Open these four paycheck jobs before your next deposit.
9. The problem is not always overspending. Sometimes it is timing.
10. Income is not the same thing as available cash.

## Long-Form Content

1. The Paycheck Map: How To Stop Feeling Broke Before Payday
2. The Money Leak Finder: A 30-Minute Cash-Flow Audit
3. Why Your Checking Account Makes Your Life Look More Affordable Than It Is
4. The Monthly Money Meeting Every Freelancer Should Run
5. How To Build Your First $1,000 Emergency Buffer Without Guessing
6. Debt Snowball vs Avalanche: How To Choose Without Losing Momentum
7. The Four Jobs Every Paycheck Needs

## Trust-Building Posts

- Show a fake paycheck and split it into bills, debt, safety, and life.
- Break down a subscription audit and show how small recurring charges stack up.
- Tell a story about a person who earned enough but had no bill buffer.
- Record a screen walkthrough of the Money Leak Finder.
- Post a payday checklist with one action step.

## Anti-AI Voice Rules

- Use specific numbers and concrete situations.
- Use sentence fragments occasionally.
- Say "I see this all the time" only when tied to a real pattern.
- Avoid empty motivational language.
- Write like a person explaining something across a desk.
